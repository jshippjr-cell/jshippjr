## 1. Contradictions between deliverables

**C1 · Who sends touch one, to whom, how fast.** 04 §3: *"Fifteen a week: eight Tuesday,
seven Thursday. Weeks 1–2 the thirty, nobody else; weeks 3–6 the sixty slots."* 06 §1: *"Of
the brief's fifteen connections a week, five are touch one and ten go to m03's sixty-name
list."* 06 §4: *"Five sequences a week … thirty in sequence by the end of week six."* Under
04 the thirty are all at day 0 by week 2, thirty Read offers land in week 4 (06 caps Reads at
three a week) and thirty touch-fives in week 8. 04 also writes its own touch-one sentences
(§3) while 06 §2 writes the sequence's day-0 text. **06 wins**: the sequence owns day 0, five
starts a week, 06's text; 04's sentences survive for seats never sequenced. The roadmap's
*"Ten new sequences a week"* (§13) is stale — §7.

**C2 · Three list sizes.** 06 §1 builds forty (tiers A–C). 04 §5 builds *sixty* slots of
which 1–34 are agency producers and EPs — the seats 06 sequences. 05 §7 cuts *"sixty names"*
outright; roadmap §07–12 keeps them. **Ruling:** one register. 06's forty is the sequence
list; 04's slots 35–60 (counsel, supervisors, editors, chapter staff, PRO reps, trade
editors) are the no-ask referral seats; slots 1–34 fold into 06 §1 as sources. 04 records
people in `buyer_person`, 06 in `decision_makers`; ADR-0050 makes `buyer_person` the person
— 08 states which row the sheet reads.

**C3 · Business affairs, cold.** 04 §3 and §5 slots 35–38 request *"Business affairs /
legal"*; 06 §1 tier C: *"Never sequenced cold. Reached after a producer at their company has
replied."* **06 wins** at any agency on the forty; 04's counsel slots are speakers and
bylines only.

**C4 · Silence.** 04 §3: *"thirty days' silence is a no; a no holds six months."* 06 §4:
*"Silence is not a no"*; §2: no acceptance by day 7 → the sequence *pauses*. **06 wins.**
The six-month hold is for a spoken no only.

**C5 · Founder hours do not add up.** 04 §2 puts Tuesday at 40 minutes for *"Comments · 8
connection requests · community 1 (30, not LinkedIn)"* — 20 of comments plus a 30-minute
sitting is already 50, and 04 §5 allows five minutes per hand-typed request, so eight are up
to 40. Honest Tuesday ≈ 90, Thursday ≈ 85; the week is ≈ 6.8 h, not *"≈ 5.2"*. Add 03 §2's
*"about one a week"* → ≈ 7.8. That leaves nothing for 07 §2's six pillars, four downloads and
front-door rewrite, five of which 02 §6 dates by week 10; 05 §1's luncheons and mixers are
counted nowhere. 06's touches and Reads (~5 h) are selling hours by 08 §1's definition,
correctly outside the eight. **Verdict:** the eight is breached in any week a page ships, and
Ruling 5's build-versus-selling test fails in weeks 1–4 unless the site work is done by the
CEO seat's session, not the founder (a ruling, §6).

**C6 · The three m02 artefacts are three different lists.** Roadmap m02: *"Certificate
walkthrough (AURORA), the room in 90s, the manifest opened on camera, the licence-terms
glossary"*. 02 §3 *"The three assets"*: pricing explainer, objection FAQ, room walkthrough.
08 §1 counts *"saves + reposts on the three m02 artefacts"* without naming them. The
roadmap's certificate walkthrough in weeks 3–4 contradicts 02 §6, which holds the certificate
recording to week 11 *"only once brief §8.3 lands"*. **02 wins on the certificate; roadmap
loses its list.** m02 = the glossary (D1), the written walkthrough (02 §3.3), Rec 2 *The
room in 90 seconds* (wk 4). 08 names those three.

**C7 · Is a band ever public?** 02 §3.1: *"States no number; the proposal is the only place a
client sees one."* 04 Notebook 2: *"a figure on a post is a rate card by another name."* 07
§5.6 records a founder ruling that `/start` *"never shows"* the band. Against that: 02 §1.6

no figure.

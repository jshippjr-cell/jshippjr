## How this list is kept at forty

§1's weekly rule stands: Friday, twenty minutes, add up to five that pass, retire any whose
sequence closed or seat changed. Three sources do the work, in this order.

1. **Ads of the World campaign pages** (`adsoftheworld.com/campaigns/<slug>`). The agency index
   pages are the way in; the campaign page is where the credit box lives, and it is the only
   free source I found that routinely prints *Head of Production*, *Executive Producer* and
   *Senior Producer* beside the creative names, with a publication month you can check against the
   twenty-four-month rule. Read the agency index for a Southeast shop, open anything dated inside
   the window, and take the production block. It is inconsistent — perhaps one campaign in three
   carries a full box — which is why it is read weekly rather than mined once.
2. **The national American Advertising Awards winners database**
   (`americanadvertisingawards.com/winners/`, entries at `/awards/<slug>/`). Every entry page gives
   the entrant company **and its city**, which is the field the trade press almost never prints, plus
   the full credit list. Filter to Film and to Video & Sound, and to the current year, and the
   Southeast entrants surface with their production seats attached.
3. **The chapter ADDY galleries that publish credits, starting with Orlando**
   (`orlandoaddys.com/winners/<year>/category/film-video-sound/`). This is the single best Southeast
   source in the set — it publishes every credited name and role for every winner, by category, by
   year, and it refreshes each February. Most chapters publish only a PDF winners book or a company
   list; Orlando publishes the credits. Charlotte, Tampa Bay, Nashville and Atlanta are worth
   re-checking each February in case they follow.

A fourth is worth watching but is not yet a source: SHOOT ceased publication in August 2026, so its
credit boxes are an archive now, not a feed. Little Black Book and The Drum both refuse automated
reads, so anything found there has to be opened by hand before a name enters the list.

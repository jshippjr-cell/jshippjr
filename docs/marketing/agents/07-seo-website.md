# SEO & Website

**Reports to:** the CEO. **Deliverable:** `docs/marketing/deliverables/07-seo-website.md`.
**Wave 2** — independent research; runs in parallel with Content, Community and Analytics.

## Charter
A long-term SEO strategy. The topics agencies search before hiring a music partner.
Pillar pages, blog ideas, downloadable resources, evergreen educational content. Everything
reinforces authority.

## Where you start
`docs/marketing/00-brief.md` and the roadmap's §14. The site is `chordential.com`, served
from this repository (`src/chordential_oia/web/public.py`, templates under
`templates/public/`). Read what the front door says today before proposing what it
should say.

## What your deliverable must contain
1. The query map: the questions a producer types, grouped by intent, with the page that
   should answer each. No invented search volumes; if you cite a number, cite its source.
2. The six pillar pages in build order, each with: title, the question it answers, its
   outline to the H2, the download it carries, the internal links.
3. Twenty supporting posts, titled, each mapped to a pillar and a series.
4. The four downloadables, specified to the section. Ungated in year one; say why.
5. The front-door audit: what `/`, `/capabilities` and `/start` say now, what contradicts
   the brief, and the rewrite.
6. Technical: what the site needs for the above to rank (structure, metadata, speed) —
   only what you have verified against the actual templates.
7. What you cut and why.

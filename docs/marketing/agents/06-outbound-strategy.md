# Outbound Strategy

**Reports to:** the CEO. **Deliverable:** `docs/marketing/deliverables/06-outbound-strategy.md`.
**Wave 3** — reads Brand, Content and Community deliverables first.

## Charter
A repeatable, relationship-first outbound system. Who to contact, when, how often, in
what sequence. The first message. The second message. How value is provided before
anything is asked.

## Where you start
`docs/marketing/00-brief.md`, the roadmap's §13, and the prior seats' work absorbed
there: `docs/sales-playbook.md` (objection framework and guardrails) and
`docs/relationship-strategy.md`. The brief's five-touch sequence and the **Clearance
Read** offer are the CEO's decisions; build on them.

## What your deliverable must contain
1. The list-building method: how the first 40 producers are found from public credits,
   and the rule that keeps the list current.
2. The sequence: every touch, its day, its channel, its full text — five messages, each
   with the variable slots marked.
3. The Clearance Read, operationalised: the intake, the one-page template returned, the
   hour it costs, the follow-up rule.
4. Cadence and limits: sequences started per week, the "a no is a no for six months"
   rule, what is logged where (this studio runs on ChordOS; say what the outreach record
   should capture so win/loss learns from it).
5. The objection responses, in the founder's voice, for the six objections the sales
   playbook ranked highest.
6. What you cut and why.

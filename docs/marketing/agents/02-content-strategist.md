# Content Strategist

**Reports to:** the CEO. **Deliverable:** `docs/marketing/deliverables/02-content-strategist.md`.
**Wave 2** — reads the Brand Strategist's deliverable first.

## Charter
Design the one-year content strategy and the recurring series. Create educational
content people would actively save and share.

## Where you start
`docs/marketing/00-brief.md`, then `docs/marketing/deliverables/01-brand-strategist.md`,
then the two prior seats' work already absorbed into the brief: `docs/content-strategy.md`
and `docs/editorial-operating-system.md`. They proposed 7 pillars, 12 series and a
7-tier cadence; the brief cut that to six series and three posts a week, and says why.
Work under the brief's cut. If you believe a cut series should live, argue it under
"Objection to the brief".

## What your deliverable must contain
1. The six series, each with: cadence, format, what a producer saves it for, the first
   four episodes titled and one-line briefed.
2. The quarterly themes and what ships in each.
3. The three assets the sales playbook deferred to you — a pricing explainer, an
   objection FAQ, a room walkthrough — specified to the paragraph.
4. The editorial standard: the checklist every piece passes before it publishes.
5. The repurposing cascade: one asset → LinkedIn / YouTube / Instagram / X, with what
   changes for each.
6. A 90-day calendar, week by week, from the Monday after this document lands.
7. What you cut from the prior documents and why.

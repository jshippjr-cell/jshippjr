# Brand Strategist

**Reports to:** the CEO. **Deliverable:** `docs/marketing/deliverables/01-brand-strategist.md`.
**Wave 1** — you go first and alone; every other role reads your output.

## Charter
Define and continuously refine brand positioning, messaging, taglines, narrative,
differentiation, value proposition and competitive positioning. Correct inconsistencies
before they reach the public.

## Where you start
Not from zero. Read, in this order: `docs/marketing/00-brief.md` (the canon),
`docs/brand/BRAND_FOUNDATION.md` and `docs/messaging-bible.md` (two prior seats' work,
absorbed into the brief), and `docs/marketing/roadmap.md`. Your job is to **resolve, not
re-derive**: where the prior documents disagree, the brief records the CEO's ruling; where
the brief leaves a decision open, you make a recommendation with the reasoning.

## What your deliverable must contain
1. The positioning statement, final, one paragraph.
2. The line, and the three alternates, with the context each is for (site, business
   affairs, a producer's inbox).
3. The three pillars (Original · Cleared · Delivered), each with its proof surface.
4. The vocabulary: use / never, extended from the brief, with the reason for each addition.
5. Competitive positioning against libraries, incumbent music houses, and machine-made
   music — without naming a peer.
6. The narrative: 150 words a producer could repeat to a creative director.
7. **Inconsistency audit:** every place the public site, the product copy, the call
   scripts and the prior documents disagree with the brief. List them; propose the fix.
8. Objections to the brief, if any, under their own heading.

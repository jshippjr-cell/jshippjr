# LinkedIn Growth

**Reports to:** the CEO. **Deliverable:** `docs/marketing/deliverables/04-linkedin-growth.md`.
**Wave 3** — reads Brand, Content and Community deliverables first.

## Charter
Redesign the founder's LinkedIn presence: headline, About, Experience, Featured, banner,
profile image, posting cadence, connection strategy, comment strategy, thought-leadership
strategy. A weekly publishing schedule. Who to follow and meaningfully engage with.

## Where you start
`docs/marketing/00-brief.md`, then deliverables 01, 02 and 05. The brief drafts no
headline or About (an earlier pointer to "§05" was wrong); the deliverable's §1 is the
draft of record, and the Brand Strategist's producer line is its headline.

## What your deliverable must contain
1. Headline (final), About (final, full text), Experience entries (final text), Featured
   (three items, in order), banner and photo direction.
2. The weekly schedule, day by day, aligned to the Content Strategist's calendar.
3. The connection doctrine: who, how many a week, the sentence that goes with each.
4. The comment doctrine: where, how long, what counts as adding something.
5. The first 60 people to engage with — **by public credit, not from memory**: award
   credits, association rosters, trade-press bylines. Each with the public thing that put
   them on the list. No private individuals named on recollection alone.
6. The founder's first twelve posts, written out.

# Analytics

**Reports to:** the CEO. **Deliverable:** `docs/marketing/deliverables/08-analytics.md`.
**Wave 2** — runs in parallel with Content, Community and SEO.

## Charter
Define success metrics: brand awareness, website traffic, discovery calls, LinkedIn
growth, newsletter subscribers, inbound leads, content engagement, referral traffic,
organic search. Measure what matters.

## Where you start
`docs/marketing/00-brief.md` and the roadmap's §15. Followers are not on the dashboard.
The two numbers the CEO watches hardest are **Clearance Reads requested** and **replies
from target roles**. The studio runs on ChordOS, which already counts discovery calls,
signed Discovery Summaries and every send in the outbox — read
`docs/architecture/PROJECT_STATE.md` for what is already measured before proposing
anything new.

## What your deliverable must contain
1. The dashboard: two rows, lagging outcomes and leading signals, each metric with its
   definition, its source, and who records it.
2. The weekly sheet: columns, the Friday fifteen-minute routine, the thresholds that
   trigger a stop decision (the roadmap's m06 rule: no leading signal in 8 weeks → stop).
3. Source attribution: how every inbound and every discovery call gets a source field, and
   the vocabulary for it.
4. What NOT to measure, and why each vanity metric is excluded.
5. What ChordOS should record that it does not yet, as a short, prioritised list for the
   CEO (who also holds the codebase).
6. The quarterly review format, including the numbers the studio publishes about itself.

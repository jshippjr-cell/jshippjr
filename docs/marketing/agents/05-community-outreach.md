# Community & Outreach

**Reports to:** the CEO. **Deliverable:** `docs/marketing/deliverables/05-community-outreach.md`.
**Wave 2** — independent research; runs in parallel with Content, SEO and Analytics.

## Charter
Research where the audience already spends time. Free opportunities first: advertising,
producer, commercial-production and music-supervision communities; Slack, Discord, Reddit,
Facebook and LinkedIn groups; newsletters; associations (AMP, AICP and peers);
conferences; virtual events; podcasts; guest-article opportunities. For each: who is
there, why it matters, how to participate without spamming, what Chordential contributes.
Long-term relationships over promotion.

## Where you start
`docs/marketing/00-brief.md` and the roadmap's §07–§12, plus the prior seats' work
absorbed there: `docs/growth-engine.md` and `docs/relationship-strategy.md`.

## What your deliverable must contain
1. The venue table: every venue, one row, the five columns from the charter, plus
   **(verify)** on anything whose current details you have not confirmed. Do not invent
   a community, show or event.
2. The two communities to start in this month and why those two.
3. The contribution doctrine: the first ten contributions in any room carry no link and no
   pitch; what counts as a contribution.
4. The guest-article pitch list: five outlets, five titles, the seed for each.
5. The podcast list by category, with the pitch angle per category.
6. Speaking: the one talk, its outline, and the venues in order of ease.
7. What you cut and why.

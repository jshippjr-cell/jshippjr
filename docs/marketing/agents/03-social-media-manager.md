# Social Media Manager

**Reports to:** the CEO. **Deliverable:** `docs/marketing/deliverables/03-social-media-manager.md`.
**Wave 3** — reads Brand, Content, Community and SEO deliverables first.

## Charter
Platform-specific strategies for LinkedIn, Instagram, X, YouTube and TikTok, each with
its own tone, cadence and content style. A rolling 90-day calendar. Every post supports
the long-term positioning. No engagement bait. Create authority.

## Where you start
`docs/marketing/00-brief.md`, then deliverables 01, 02, 05 and 07. LinkedIn is the primary
surface and has its own seat (04); you own the other four and the cross-platform calendar,
and you coordinate with 04 rather than duplicating it.

## What your deliverable must contain
1. Per platform: role, tone, cadence, formats, what never goes there.
2. The rolling 90-day calendar, merged with the Content Strategist's, per platform.
3. The first ten posts for each platform, written out, with the series each belongs to.
4. The comment and reply doctrine per platform.
5. The TikTok decision: a Q3 test with a stop condition, or a reasoned no.
6. What you cut and why.

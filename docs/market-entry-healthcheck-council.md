## 0. What we reviewed

**The research thesis (the spine of everything):** *human-authored,
rights-clearable music is now the scarce premium good* — because AI flooded the
commodity tier (Deezer: ~44% of daily uploads are AI) **and** AI-only output
**cannot be copyrighted, licensed, insured, or defended** (US Copyright Office, 2025).
The sharpest wedge inside that: the **"creator licensing trap"** — blanket/library
licenses die the moment music crosses channels or sits behind **paid spend**, and
licensed tracks still trip **YouTube Content ID**. The research's prescription:

- **Beachhead (Roadmap A, ~90 days to revenue):** *Clearance-Certified* custom music
  for brands & agencies — **3 fixed-scope packages** (Social Sprint ~$2.5k / Campaign
  ~$6k / Brand Anthem + sonic logo ~$15k) wrapped in a **Clearance Certificate**
  (full ownership, cross-channel + paid-spend license, Content-ID safelist,
  **indemnification**). Sell to agencies as a reseller channel; convert pilots to
  **retainers (MRR)**. Gate: 5 agencies / **$15k MRR**.
- Recurring layer (C) and the indie-game niche (B) come *after* A throws cash.

**What we've actually built:** a genuinely excellent **internal operating system** —
identify → rank → qualify → strategic-value → estimate → prepare → outreach → win/loss
→ buyer graph, plus (this stretch) a consolidated back-office, an editable
capabilities/delivery document, a first-touch composer + tailored page, tactful
intake, talent recruiting + per-talent rates, and a polished public site with Stripe
invoicing.

## 2. The diagnosis — where we've drifted (say it plainly)

**CRO (lead — the hard truth):** We have built a superb machine for *processing* deal
flow we **do not yet have**, and a delivery experience for a **product we have not yet
packaged or sold.** The research's beachhead is a *productized offer sold into agencies*
— 90 days to revenue. We've spent the cycles on **tooling and craft**, not on a **named,
priced, buyable product** and a **motion to put it in front of agencies.** The plumbing
is gorgeous; **the faucet isn't connected to a customer.**

**CMO:** Sharper still — **the single most defensible thing in the research is barely
productized.** The wedge is *"music your legal team can defend — Content-ID-safe,
licensed across every channel and paid spend, indemnified."* That should be the **name
of the product and the headline of the site.** Instead we lead with "premium craft,"
which is true but *undifferentiated* — every boutique says it. We're sitting on the
one claim AI can't make and under-using it.

**Head of Production:** And here's the operational gap behind the marketing: **can we
actually deliver the Clearance Certificate today?** Documented chain of title, a
Content-ID safelist, a paid-spend-safe license, **indemnification** — that's a
*real legal + ops capability*, not a PDF section. If we sell it before we can deliver
it, the wedge becomes a liability. We built a beautiful *delivery package*; we have not
confirmed we can stand behind the *clearance guarantee* that makes it sellable.

**CFO:** Capital-discipline flag: **the supply side ran ahead of the roadmap's own
sequencing.** The roadmap says supply is *"Later — only after the demand side is trusted
and we are winning enough work to staff."* We just built talent recruiting, tiers, and
per-talent rate→proposal math. That's staffing a pipeline **we can't yet fill** — the
exact thing the founder's own ratified strategy warned against. Reasonable as
*scaffolding*, premature as *investment*.

**Founder's Advocate (counter, for balance):** Careful labeling it all "drift." A new
studio with no logos needs the polished doc and site to be *credible enough to win the
first pilot* — craft **is** part of the GTM here. The miss isn't that we built quality;
it's that we **haven't pointed it at a buyer.** The work is a loaded gun with no target
selected.

**CRO:** Agreed — that's the precise diagnosis. **No demand-gen motion exists.** The OS
qualifies inbound; there is **no defined way leads arrive.** The research is explicit:
*relationship-pitch ad agencies & production companies as a reseller channel.* That's an
outbound, founder-led sales motion — and it isn't built, scheduled, or being measured.

**CTO:** And we're **not measuring against the research's gates** (3–5 pilots, $15k MRR).
We measure *features shipped and tests green*. Healthy for engineering; silent on the
only number that matters pre-PMF: **is anyone paying?**

## 3. The pivot the cabinet recommends (for the founder to rule on)

A one-line reframe: **stop building the factory; sell the first order.** Concretely:

1. **Productize the offer.** Turn Clearance-Certified into a **named product** with the
   3 fixed packages, prices, scope, and the **Certificate + indemnification** as the
   hero — buyable from the site (pick → deposit via the wired Stripe → start).
2. **Re-headline on the wedge.** Lead with *legal-defensibility / Content-ID-safe /
   follows-the-asset-across-paid-spend*, not generic premium craft.
3. **Confirm we can actually deliver the clearance guarantee** (chain of title,
   safelist, indemnification) — or scope it down to what we *can* stand behind.
4. **Build the demand motion:** a named list of target agencies + a founder-led outbound
   pitch (reuse the first-touch composer for *agency* outreach), and measure against the
   pilot/MRR gates — not feature count.
5. **Freeze further supply-side investment** until the demand side is winning work, per
   the roadmap's own sequencing.

This is a recommendation, not a ruling. The founder's answers below set the direction.

## 10 questions for the founder

*Sharp, decision-forcing, ordered from "what are we selling" → "to whom" → "are we
ready" → "what's the line." Answer these and the GTM writes itself.*

1. **Which product goes to market first — the music *service* (Clearance-Certified
   packages) or the *software/OS* itself?** The strategy says the service (dogfood the
   studio = Model A); the OS-as-a-product is a later Model-C / metadata-tooling play.
   Confirm which "product" you mean, because everything downstream forks here.

2. **Are you ready to name, price, and *publish* the three packages** (Social Sprint
   ~$2.5k / Campaign ~$6k / Brand Anthem + sonic logo ~$15k) as things a buyer can pick
   and pay a deposit on this month — or do those scopes/prices need to change first?

3. **Will you bet the brand on the legal-defensibility wedge?** Lead everything —
   headline, site, pitch — with *"music your legal team can defend: Content-ID-safe,
   licensed across every channel and all paid spend, indemnified"* instead of "premium
   craft." Yes or no — and if no, what's the differentiator you'd lead with instead?

4. **Can you actually deliver the Clearance Certificate today** — documented chain of
   title, a Content-ID safelist, a paid-spend-safe license, and **indemnification** — or
   is part of that still aspirational? What specifically is missing to make the guarantee
   real and safe to sell?

5. **Who is the first buyer, by name?** Can you list 3–5 specific agencies or production
   companies you could pitch *this week* (the reseller channel the research prescribes)?
   If not, is your honest first market actually direct-to-brand, or somewhere else?

6. **Do we pause the supply side?** Your own roadmap puts talent/recruiting as "Later —
   only after demand is trusted." Do you want to **freeze further supply-side build** and
   pour everything into landing paying clients — or do you believe you need a vetted bench
   ready *before* you can credibly sell?

7. **Define "launched."** What single outcome, 90 days out, means we've begun offering a
   product to the market — 3 signed pilots? $15k MRR? A live buyable package page with
   the first deposit collected? Pick the one gate we measure against.

8. **Will you sell retainers, and can you deliver them?** The research's revenue
   smoother is converting pilots to MRR. Are you prepared to sell an ongoing retainer —
   and do you (plus one contractor) have the delivery capacity to honor it without
   drowning?

9. **Is the recurring brand-safe / stream-safe subscription (Roadmap C) on or off the
   table for year one** — funded later from A's cash, or a distraction you want explicitly
   parked until the beachhead is paying?

10. **What is the one thing you are *not* willing to do to reach revenue?** Cold-pitch
    agencies? Sell rigid fixed-scope packages? Discount the first pilots? Take
    work-for-hire that doesn't build the moat? Naming the hard constraint tells us which
    GTM is actually open to you — and which to stop proposing.

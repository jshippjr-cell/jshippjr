# Opportunity Intelligence: Music Production for Screen, Games & Advertising

*Deep-research briefing for Chordential — June 2026*

> Methodology: five parallel research streams (market sizing, top players, money
> flow, structural shifts, startup gaps/GTM), each returning falsifiable, cited
> claims, then cross-verified. Confidence flags are preserved throughout.

## 1. The honest market map

The sync/production-music market is real and growing, but **much smaller than
vendor reports claim**. See through the definitional fog.

| Segment | Credible size (2024) | Growth | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Global recorded-music sync** | **$650M** (+6.4%) | high single digit | **High** — IFPI |
| Total recorded music | $29.6B (IFPI) / $36.2B (MIDiA) | +4.8–6.5% | High |
| Stock / production music | ~$1.45B → $2.4B by 2030 | ~8.8% CAGR | Medium |
| Royalty-free music | ~$1.43B → $2.0B by 2030 | ~5.9% CAGR | Medium |
| Game music | ~$1.4B | 7.7–12.4% | Low-medium |
| Sonic / audio branding | ~$1.1–2.1B | ~10–14% | Low |
| Generative AI music | ~$0.4–0.57B → ~$2.1–2.8B by 2030 | **~30% CAGR** | Projection |
| Total music industry | ~$100B → **~$200B by 2035** | Goldman Sachs | High on source |

When vendors quote a "$5.8B music licensing market," they bundle publishing,
performance royalties, and loose definitions. The clean, comparable figure for
*recorded-music sync* is IFPI's **$650M**. The opportunity is not raw size — it
is the **~30% growth of AI music colliding with a premium market that still
requires human authorship.**

## 2. Who owns the space

**Production music libraries (catalog scale, subscription):**
- **Epidemic Sound** — $181.6M revenue 2024 (+29%), ~250k owned tracks, played
  2.5B times/day on YouTube/TikTok, $1.4B valuation (2021). *Still unprofitable —
  scale alone is not winning.*
- **Artlist** — ~$199–299/yr, 30M+ creators, cash-flow positive since 2016, pays
  artists 30% of net.
- **APM Music** (Sony/Universal JV, ~1.1M tracks), **Universal Production Music**,
  **Audio Network** (SESAC/Blackstone), **Pond5** (Shutterstock, $210M, 2022).

**Sync / licensing marketplaces (B2B roll-up):**
- **Songtradr** — the consolidator. Rolled up Big Sync, MassiveMusic, 7digital
  ($23.4M), and **Bandcamp** (from Epic, 2023); unified B2B arms under
  MassiveMusic in 2025.
- **Lickd** (pre-cleared major-label music for YouTubers, ~$8/song, Warner + Epic
  backed), **Tracklib** (legal sampling, unlimited clearances from $14.99/mo),
  **Marmoset**, **Musicbed**.

**Audio / sonic branding:** Sixième Son, MassiveMusic, Made Music Studio,
antfood, amp.

**Game audio:** Wwise/Audiokinetic (Sony-owned, 500+ titles/yr), FMOD —
middleware most generic libraries cannot service.

**AI music:** Suno ($5.4B valuation, June 2026), Udio (a16z-backed), Soundraw,
AIVA, Loudly, Mubert (B2B API). Majors now licensing: UMG/Udio (Oct 2025),
Warner/Suno (Nov 2025); Sony still litigating, pivotal fair-use ruling expected
**summer 2026.**

**Tooling proof-points (acquirer interest):** SoundCloud bought **Musiio** (AI
tagging, 2022); **Beatdapp** (fraud detection) raised $17M, partnered with
UMG/SoundExchange.

## 3. How the money flows (so you price right)

- **Sync = one-time upfront license**, usually "all-in" covering master +
  publishing, split ~50/50 under **MFN parity**. It does *not* cover performance
  royalties — those flow via **PROs (ASCAP/BMI/SESAC)** off **cue sheets** when
  content airs. No cue sheet, no backend.
- **Fee anchors:** local/regional ad ~$1–25k; national TV commercial ~$15k–250k+;
  global chart-track campaign $500k–7-figures. Indie film $1–10k/song; studio
  film $10–100k. **Indie games ~$500–2,000/track.** Network TV episode ~$1–10k.
- **Library economics:** libraries take ~30–50% of sync fees. Epidemic-style
  **buyout**: ~$2k–8k upfront per track, owns all rights, no PRO registration.
- **Music supervision:** ~10–20% of music budget; TV ~$2–5k/episode; mid-budget
  film ~$15–20k.
- **The creator licensing trap (the wedge):** platform in-app libraries
  (TikTok/Meta) only cover *native* posts. Cross-channel reposts or **paid spend**
  behind UGC kill the blanket license. "Royalty-free" waives royalties, **not
  copyright** — licensed tracks still trigger **YouTube Content ID** claims.
  Twitch's 3-strike DMCA escalates to permanent bans.

## 4. The structural shifts creating openings

1. **AI bifurcated the market.** Deezer (April 2026): **~44% of daily uploads —
   ~75,000 tracks/day — are fully AI-generated** (up from ~10k/day a year prior),
   yet they earn only **1–3% of streams.** Commodity music is going to zero.
   Meanwhile the **US Copyright Office (Jan 2025) + federal appeals court (Mar
   2025)** held **AI-only output cannot be copyrighted** — so it cannot be cleanly
   licensed, insured, or defended. *Human-authored, rights-clearable music is now
   the scarce, premium good.* This is the most important fact in this report.
2. **Demand is migrating to Chordential's strengths.** Steam hit **~19,100 game
   releases in 2025** (≈99% indie, most with no music contacts), while scripted
   "Peak TV" is *contracting* (~31% YoY drop). Short-form/branded video needs
   fast, cleared, multi-version music — **24–72hr turnaround for social.**
3. **Persistent unsolved pain:** ~**$2.5B** in unclaimed "black-box" royalties
   from bad metadata; Content ID misfires; fragmented per-platform clearance.
4. **Consolidation = exit paths.** Songtradr, SoundCloud, Shutterstock, and the
   majors are all *acquiring*. Niche tools and curated catalogs get bought.

## 5. Unique, defensible startup ideas

Filtered for ideas that (a) ride the AI-scarcity wedge, (b) complement an agile
original-music + supervision studio rather than require catalog scale, and (c)
have evidence of demand or acquirer interest.

**Idea 1 — "Clearance-Certified" custom music for brands & agencies (beachhead).**
Productize what AI *cannot* sell: a guaranteed-clearable, Content-ID-safe,
fully-owned music package with documented chain of title, stems, cutdowns, and a
license that follows the asset across every channel and paid spend. Defensibility
= trust, speed, indemnification — not catalog size. Price $2k–25k.

**Idea 2 — Indie-game adaptive scoring, productized.**
~19,100 games/year, ≈99% indie, no music contacts, need **Wwise/FMOD adaptive
integration** generic libraries do not offer. Tiered packages, obvious acquirer
pool.

**Idea 3 — Brand-safe music ops for creators/streamers (recurring revenue).**
Twitch/YouTube DMCA pain is permanent. Songtradr validated it by buying Pretzel
(MRR +600% YoY). Curated, human-made, stream-safe catalog as subscription + a
"Vouch"-style Content-ID pre-empt tool (à la Lickd).

**Idea 4 — Metadata & chain-of-title as a service.**
$2.5B black-box problem; SoundCloud bought Musiio, Beatdapp raised $17M on
adjacent pain. Auto-generate clean cue sheets, stems manifests, clearance docs —
first for your own deliveries, then sold to other studios.

**Idea 5 — Curated human-made antidote catalog ("Certified Human").**
As 44% of uploads go AI, a small certified-human, brand-safe catalog with
provenance guarantees is a trust/marketing wedge bolted onto Ideas 1–3.

**Recommendation:** Idea 1 is the beachhead, Idea 3 the recurring-revenue layer,
Idea 2 the high-margin niche. Ideas 4–5 are moats added once volume exists.

## 6. Launch roadmaps

### Roadmap A — Clearance-Certified Campaign Music (beachhead, ~90 days to revenue)
- **MVP (Wks 1–4):** 3 fixed-scope packages (*Social Sprint* 1 track + 3 cutdowns
  + stems, 72hr, ~$2.5k; *Campaign* ~$6k; *Brand Anthem + sonic logo* ~$15k).
  Build the **Clearance Certificate** (full ownership, cross-channel + paid-spend
  license, Content-ID safelist, indemnification). Standardize intake →
  revision-cap → delivery.
- **Launch (Wks 5–10):** Sell to ad agencies & production companies as a reseller
  channel (relationship pitching). Land 3–5 pilots. Hook: "music your legal team
  can defend."
- **Growth (Mo 3–9):** Convert pilots to **retainers** (MRR). Partner portal,
  clearance/Content-ID content marketing, one composer-contractor.
- **Gates:** MVP→Launch when packages + certificate live; Launch→Growth at 5
  agencies / $15k MRR.

### Roadmap B — Indie-Game Scoring Studio (highest margin, ~4–6 months)
- **MVP (Wks 1–6):** Pick a vertical (cozy/narrative). 2 portfolio scores **with
  Wwise integration.** Package: Theme Pack / Full Adaptive Score / Score +
  Middleware Integration.
- **Launch (Wks 7–16):** itch.io, Steam Next Fest, r/gamedev, IndieCade,
  game-audio Discords. Offer perpetuity/buyout licenses indies *cannot* get from
  libraries.
- **Growth (Mo 4–12):** Repeatable Wwise pipeline, revenue-share on promising
  titles, shipped games as case studies.

### Roadmap C — Brand-Safe Subscription (recurring revenue, ~6 months, fund from A)
- **MVP (Mo 1–2):** Curate a *small* certified-human, stream-safe catalog
  (100–200 tracks). Subscription ~$10–20/mo.
- **Launch (Mo 3–4):** Sell to streamers/creators with a **Content-ID safelist
  guarantee** (Lickd "Vouch" mechanic). Partner with mid-tier streamers.
- **Growth (Mo 5–12):** Auto-claim-release tool, tiers, acquirer narrative.
  Transparent composer revenue share (Artlist's 30% model).

**Sequencing:** Run **A now** (existing business, productized + contractual moat).
Fund **C** from A's cash flow (smooths project lumpiness). Add **B** once delivery
is systematized. Layer in metadata tooling (Idea 4) when volume justifies it.

## 7. Confidence & caveats

- **High confidence:** IFPI sync/recorded figures; AI lawsuit/settlement timeline;
  Copyright Office rulings; Deezer's 44% upload stat; Steam release counts;
  funding/acquisition facts (MBW/TechCrunch-sourced).
- **Directional only:** all market size/CAGR figures for game music, sonic
  branding, stock/royalty-free, and AI music come from second-tier syndicated
  vendors that conflict. The *trend* (high-single to low-double digit growth; AI
  ~30%) is reliable; the absolute dollars are not.
- Sync fees vary widely by fame/term/territory — use as bands, not quotes.

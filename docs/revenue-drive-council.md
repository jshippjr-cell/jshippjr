# Revenue Drive — Council (CRO-led): turn the OS into USD

*Mandate from the founder: stop optimizing the machine and start driving **usage** and
**actual dollars (USD)**. The CRO leads. The team's job is not to agree — it's to find
the shortest honest path to the first real paid project and a repeatable second, third,
tenth. Decisions for Jon to rule on are collected at the end.*

**Room:** CRO (lead), Founder/CEO (Jon), CFO, CMO, COO, CTO, Head of Production,
Founder's Advocate (honesty).

---

## 0. The CRO opens — the brutal frame

**CRO:** Let's be honest about where we are. We have built a *remarkable* operating
system — discovery, qualification, scoring, estimation, proposals, a Frame.io-grade
delivery portal, clearance certificates, cue sheets, a composer portal, a payout ledger,
and now a payment gate. **And we have made $0.** Every one of those is a cost center
until a customer pays. My job today is to flip the company's center of gravity from
*"what can the machine do"* to *"who wrote us a check and why."*

Three numbers matter and we have none of them: **(1) dollars collected, (2) paying
projects delivered, (3) a repeatable motion that produces #1 and #2.** Everything below
serves those.

**Founder's Advocate:** And one constraint that overrides everything — the honesty rule.
Every demo to date uses invented brands. The first case study must be a **real, paid**
engagement. No faked logos, no "trusted by," no theater. We earn the proof.

---

## 1. The diagnosis — why $0 (grounding, not blame)

**CRO:** We've been *supply- and tooling-led* when revenue is *demand-led*. The pattern:

- **We built capacity to fulfill before we created demand to fill it.** Classic founder
  trap — the OS is real, but it answers "how do we deliver?" not "who buys, and why now?"
- **The discovery scanner is bot-blocked** (the founder already flagged this). We leaned
  on an automated top-of-funnel that doesn't work, so the funnel is empty.
- **No offer.** We sell "original music" — a category, not a purchase. A buyer can't say
  yes to a category. They say yes to *a scoped thing at a price by a date.*
- **No proof.** Zero case studies, so even a warm lead has nothing to de-risk the buy.
- **The founder's own doubt** (on record): *"I don't think we're different enough... we
  don't have a proof of concept."* That doubt is the real blocker — and it's solvable
  only by **transacting**, not by building more.

**CFO:** Agreed, and I'll add the money truth: we have infrastructure depreciation and
zero cash in. The *only* acceptable next milestone is **cash collected**, ideally a
deposit before a single note is written. We already built deposit + final invoicing and
the payment gate — we have never *used* them on a real dollar.

---

## 2. What do we actually sell, and to whom? — CMO + CRO

**CMO:** "Music" is a commodity and the founder is right to doubt our differentiation
*as a music vendor.* We are not differentiated as composers. We **are** differentiated as
a **clearance-certified, procurement-grade delivery experience.** Reframe the product:

> We don't sell music. We sell an **agency producer's worst week made painless** — original,
> rights-clean music with a signed clearance certificate, a cue sheet, organized delivery
> folders, and a review portal — so legal/business-affairs never flags it and the producer
> never chases files.

**CRO:** Right. The buyer is **not** "a brand that wants a song." It's the **agency
producer / business-affairs / brand content lead** whose pain is *rights risk + delivery
chaos.* We sell **certainty and a painless process**, and the music rides along. That's a
purchase someone signs for, because the alternative (library tracks with murky rights, or
a freelancer who delivers a bare mp3) carries real legal and time risk.

**Founder's Advocate (dissent):** Careful — we can't *over*-claim the clearance value
until we've actually defended one. The certificate is real and useful, but the pitch is
"defensible, documented, original," not "litigation-proof." Sell what we can stand behind.

**CMO:** Accepted. The wedge is **"documented, original, delivery-ready"** — risk
*reduced*, not risk *eliminated*.

---

## 3. Productize the first offer — CRO (lead) + Head of Production

**CRO:** A bespoke-quote business has a long, uncertain sales cycle — death for a studio
with no proof. We need a **productized first offer**: a fixed scope, a fixed price, a
fixed timeline, with a "Buy / Start" button and a deposit. Three candidate wedges:

| Offer | Scope | Indicative price | Why it sells |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Sonic Signature** | A short sonic logo / mnemonic (3–5s) + 2 revisions + clearance cert | **$3,500–6,000** | Small, concrete, brand-owned forever, easy yes |
| **:30 Spot Score** | Original :30 score for one spot + 1 cutdown + cert + cue sheet | **$6,000–12,000** | Maps to a real campaign deliverable |
| **Brand Anthem** | :60 hero track + stems + cutdowns + full delivery package | **$12,000–25,000** | Higher ACV, but longer cycle |

**Head of Production:** Start with the one we can **deliver on time with the roster we
actually have approved today.** A sonic logo is the lowest-risk first fulfillment — short,
fast, fewer revision cycles. Don't sell the Anthem until we've shipped a Signature.

**CFO:** And **50% deposit up front, non-refundable once work starts.** We built the
deposit invoice — use it. First dollars come *before* the composer is even assigned.

**CTO:** Concretely buildable to enable this: a **productized-offer page** (fixed tiers
→ Stripe checkout collecting the deposit → creates the project + opportunity in the OS).
We already have the Stripe seam and the project/invoice plumbing; this is wiring, not new
infrastructure. **This is the only customer-facing thing worth building right now.**

---

## 4. The first-dollar motion — CRO

**CRO:** Pipeline doesn't come from the (blocked) scanner. It comes from **founder-led
direct outbound + warm network.** The motion for the next 60 days:

1. **Target list of 30** named agency producers / brand content leads in Jon's reach
   (1st- and 2nd-degree network first — that's where the first yes lives).
2. **10 real conversations/week** — not emails into the void; calls/DMs that lead to a
   scoped offer. The recruiting outreach composer we built is the *supply* analog; we now
   need the **demand** version of that motion, run by a human (Jon).
3. **Founding-client pilots (1–3):** offer a **transparent founding rate** (e.g., 30–40%
   off the Signature) to the first few, **in exchange for a named case study + testimonial
   + a reference call.** Paid, never free. This buys the proof asset that unblocks
   everyone after them.
4. Every conversation ends with **one of three asks:** book a scoped call, send a fixed
   offer, or collect a deposit. No "let's stay in touch."

**Founder's Advocate:** "Founding rate" is honest *if* we say exactly what it is — a
launch discount for early clients who let us tell their story. Not a fake "limited time"
gimmick. And the case study must be real and approved by the client in writing.

**COO (capacity dissent):** Before we sell three pilots — **can we deliver three at once?**
We have a thin approved roster. Sell *one* founding client, deliver it flawlessly, *then*
open the next two. A blown first delivery is worse than a slow start; it poisons the only
proof we'll have.

**CRO:** Accepted — **sell one founding client, deliver it perfectly, convert it to the
case study, then scale to the next two.** Sequenced, not simultaneous.

## 5. Instrument revenue — CFO + CTO

**CFO:** We measure what we manage, and today we manage *features*, not *money*. I want a
**revenue dashboard** that shows, from the data we already store (opportunities, proposals,
invoices, payments):

- **Cash collected** (deposits + finals, USD) — the primary number.
- **Pipeline value** (qualified opps × proposal totals) and **weighted pipeline.**
- The **conversion funnel:** qualified conversations → proposals sent → deposits collected
  → delivered → paid in full.
- **Outstanding A/R** (Issued-but-unpaid invoices) — money we've earned but haven't
  collected.

**CTO:** All of that is computable from existing tables — invoices have amount/status,
proposals have totals. This is a read-only reporting view, cheap to build, and it makes
the CRO's mandate *visible* on the dashboard every day. Highest-leverage internal build.

**CRO:** That dashboard becomes the company's home screen. If a number isn't moving, we
change the action that week.

---

## 6. What we STOP — CRO + CTO

**CTO (self-critique):** The OS is *more than enough* to transact today. Continuing to add
internal features is, right now, **procrastination disguised as progress.** We stop:

- New Delivery-OS sub-features, new "agents," new internal polish.
- The bot-blocked scanner as a *primary* channel (keep it as a background nice-to-have).
- Net-new tooling that doesn't directly unblock a sale or a delivery.

**We build only two things:** (1) the **productized-offer page + deposit checkout**, and
(2) the **revenue dashboard.** Everything else waits behind the first paid dollar.

**Founder's Advocate:** And we resist the urge to build a *prettier* machine to avoid the
*scary* work — picking up the phone and asking a real person for money. The discomfort is
the job now.

## Decisions for Jon to rule on

1. **First offer / wedge.** Lead with the **Sonic Signature** ($3.5–6k, lowest fulfillment
   risk)? Or the **:30 Spot Score** (higher ACV, maps to a campaign)? *(CRO + Head of
   Production recommend starting with the Signature.)*
2. **Founding-client play.** Approve a **paid founding rate (~30–40% off) for the first
   1–3 clients in exchange for a real, client-approved case study + reference** — sold and
   delivered **one at a time** (COO's sequencing)? Or hold price firm and sell at full rate?
3. **Deposit-first cash.** Require a **50% non-refundable deposit before work starts**, via
   the Stripe checkout, on every engagement?
4. **Build focus.** Approve building **only** the two revenue enablers next — the
   **productized-offer page + deposit checkout** and the **revenue dashboard** — and a
   freeze on net-new internal features until the first dollar lands?
5. **The motion.** Commit to a founder-run **10 conversations/week** from a **named list of
   30**, with the CRO's funnel tracked on the dashboard?

*Recommendation of the room: yes to 1 (Signature first), 2 (founding rate, one at a time),
3 (deposit-first), 4 (two builds + freeze), 5 (the weekly motion). The dissents that
shaped it: CFO (cash before scale, protect the deposit), COO (deliver one before selling
three), Founder's Advocate (real paid proof only, no theater).*

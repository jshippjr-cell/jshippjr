# Chordential Master Film — Generation Kit
### "One sheet of paper" · a single uninterrupted transformation journey
### ~8 legs × 8–10s ≈ 70–80 second film · architecture A (continuous forward take)

---

## How to use this kit

1. Generate **Leg 1** from the still prompt (or straight from text).
2. **Extract the last frame** of the rendered leg (I do this for you — or any frame-grab tool).
3. Generate the next leg with that frame as the **start image** and the leg's prompt below.
4. Repeat through Leg 8. Never skip the handoff frame; never let a leg end mid-orbit.
5. Bring me the clips — I verify the seams, concatenate, slice for the scroll site,
   encode (desktop/mobile/VP9), and wire the live page.

**Rules that keep it seamless (from the scroll-world skill):**
- The ⟨STYLE PREAMBLE⟩ below is pasted **byte-for-byte identical** into every prompt.
- Every leg **ends settling into a slow, steady forward glide** (final ~1s) and every
  leg **begins by continuing that same glide** — the bolded contract lines stay verbatim.
- Orbits/crane moves are safe **inside** a leg, never across a seam.
- After each render, eyeball the last frame: it must read as a calm forward glide.
  If not, re-roll that leg before generating the next.

**⚠ The text trap (important):** video models mangle written words — "Campaign Brief,"
the version-card labels (60s / 30s / 15s), and the Chordential logo will render as
gibberish glyphs. Generate every leg **textless** (the prompts below say so), and I
composite the real text in post: the brush-written title, the version-card labels, and
the final logo + "Original Music for Campaigns · Composed. Cleared. Delivered." get
overlaid frame-accurately with your actual wordmark and a brush-reveal animation —
crisper than any model can paint, and honest to the pixel.

## ⟨STYLE PREAMBLE⟩ — paste verbatim into every prompt

> Handcrafted paper world built entirely from traditional East Asian ink wash painting,
> layered watercolor, hand-torn paper, papier-mâché, folded cardstock, textured fibers,
> natural pigments, visible brush strokes, and subtle gold flecks. Every object is
> handmade and tactile; every transformation preserves the same paper material —
> accomplished only through folding, curling, unfolding, layering, painting, stacking,
> bending, sliding, interlocking, rolling, tearing, and growing naturally from the paper.
> Nothing digital, nothing futuristic. Single continuous cinematic camera move, no cuts:
> slow floating dolly, gentle crane, subtle parallax between paper layers — calm,
> meditative, premium, never rushed or handheld. Nothing appears suddenly, nothing
> teleports, nothing dissolves; everything already exists inside what came before it,
> waiting to be revealed. Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

*(Lighting is steered per leg — soft morning light early, warm golden hour late.)*

## LEG 1 — The Sheet (soft morning light)
*Start: from text, or a still of the opening sheet.*

> ⟨STYLE PREAMBLE⟩ Soft morning light. A single sheet of textured handmade paper rests
> in a peaceful ink-wash landscape at sunrise. A calligraphy brush hovers and paints a
> short elegant stroke of wet ink across the sheet (an abstract stroke, no letters). The
> sheet begins to fold and rise into a beautifully organized document; additional pages
> emerge naturally beneath it, layering upward. **In the final second, settle into a
> slow, steady forward glide toward the growing paper layers ahead.**

## LEG 2 — The Mountain of the Brief (morning light)
*Start image: Leg 1's last frame.*

> ⟨STYLE PREAMBLE⟩ Morning light. **Continue the same slow, steady forward glide.** The
> layered pages grow into a paper mountain range. The camera flies through the valleys
> as the mountains gently unfold, revealing mood boards, handwritten-style marks,
> small photographs, color palette swatches, reference cards, timeline ribbons,
> calendar grids, and budget slips hidden within the paper layers — all drifting
> scattered and disconnected across the terrain, calm but unconnected. **In the final
> second, settle back into a slow, steady forward glide toward the widest valley
> ahead.**

## LEG 3 — The Conversation (the emotional centerpiece)
*Start image: Leg 2's last frame.*

> ⟨STYLE PREAMBLE⟩ Late-morning light. **Continue the same slow, steady forward
> glide.** The scattered paper pieces quietly begin organizing themselves: thin brush
> strokes draw connecting lines between ideas; objective cards slide toward
> deliverable cards; reference cards align into rows; timeline ribbons settle flat;
> budget slips fold gently into the central brief. Paper pieces rotate, unfold, stack,
> and interlock until the whole landscape becomes calm, ordered, clear — complexity
> becoming clarity, with no technology visible. A slow half-orbit around the newly
> organized centerpiece, keeping it centered, then continuing past it. **In the final
> second, settle back into a slow, steady forward glide toward the horizon line beyond
> the organized landscape.**

## LEG 4 — The Staff Lines (midday warmth)

## LEG 5 — The Composer's Studio (warm afternoon)
*Start image: Leg 4's last frame.*

> ⟨STYLE PREAMBLE⟩ Warm afternoon light. **Continue the same slow, steady forward

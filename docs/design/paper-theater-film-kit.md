# Chordential Agency Film — Generation Kit v3
### "The Paper Theater" · six diorama stage-rooms, one continuous flight
### Style locked to the reference image (paper-sculpture pianist)
### 6 legs × ~10s ≈ 60s · Kling (one model, ALL legs) · 16:9

---

## Workflow (one leg per day on free credits)

1. Generate Leg 1 (start image: a Scene-1 still in this style — see "Style anchor" below).
2. Send me the clip → I extract its true last frame → that's Leg 2's start image. Repeat.
3. I verify seams, composite ALL text (brief marks, seal moment, logo + tagline) with
   the real wordmark, assemble the film, and cut the scroll-site version.

**Hard rules (unchanged):** camera sentence FIRST · max two actions per leg · every leg
ends settling into a slow steady forward glide (never static, never mid-orbit) ·
⟨PREAMBLE⟩ byte-for-byte identical in every prompt · zero on-screen text (post only) ·
eyeball each last frame before generating the next.

**Style anchor:** your pianist image IS the style. Generate the Scene-1 still with the
same image tool that made it, using the ⟨PREAMBLE⟩ below + Leg 1's subject line — then
feed that still to Kling as Leg 1's start image. (Where the image tool accepts a style
reference, attach the pianist image too.)

---

## ⟨PREAMBLE⟩ — paste verbatim into every prompt

> Handcrafted paper-theater diorama: a miniature stage-box room whose walls are
> ink-wash mountain murals painted on cream parchment panels framed in slender wood
> trim. Everything inside is sculpted paper — figures built from folded cream paper
> with visible facets and fibers, furniture and objects cut from layered cardstock,
> bonsai pines and torn-paper terrain layering the foreground edges of the frame.
> Warm glowing paper lanterns light the scene; a round moon-gate window with wooden
> lattice glows softly. Palette: warm cream, parchment gold, sepia ink, muted
> sage-green mountains, lantern amber. Matte tactile paper everywhere — visible cut
> edges, soft watercolor bleeds, subtle gold flecks; nothing digital, nothing glossy.
> Ultra-smooth slow cinematic camera, floating dolly, gentle parallax between the
> torn-paper foreground layers and the mural walls, no handheld, no fast motion, no
> cuts. Paper animates only by folding, unfolding, sliding, and ink painting itself —
> nothing pops into existence. Absolutely no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos.

## LEG 1 — The Brief (morning lanterns, cool soft light)
*Start image: Scene-1 still generated per "Style anchor." Actions: pages ripple, brief rises.*

> **The camera glides slowly forward into a paper-theater conference room** — a long
> cardstock table at center stage, ink-wash mountain murals on the wall panels, two
> paper lanterns glowing gently, torn-paper terrain framing the bottom of the frame.
> ⟨PREAMBLE⟩ On the table: a bound paper brief, a folded notebook, a tiny paper coffee
> cup with a curl of paper steam. As the camera pushes in, the brief's cream pages
> turn themselves in one slow elegant ripple and the ink-wash mural behind seems to
> deepen, mountains gaining a wash of morning color. **The camera continues its slow,
> steady forward glide toward the glowing moon-gate window at the back of the room as
> the clip ends — the camera never stops moving.** Mood: quiet anticipation.

## LEG 2 — The Match (late-morning warmth)
*Start image: Leg 1's last frame. Actions: gallery reveals, ink threads connect.*

> **The camera continues the same slow, steady forward glide** through the moon-gate
> window into the next stage-box room: a curator's gallery. ⟨PREAMBLE⟩ Small framed
> paper portraits of composers hang between the mural panels; folded score scrolls
> and miniature paper instruments — a cello, a horn, a koto — rest on cardstock
> shelves, lanterns warming the space. Thin sepia ink lines paint themselves across
> the mural walls, linking one portrait to a score scroll to an instrument; that
> portrait's lantern brightens softly. Careful curation, never automation. **In the
> final second the camera settles back into a slow, steady forward glide toward a
> gap parting between two mural panels at the far end.**

## LEG 3 — The Studio (warm afternoon amber)
*Start image: Leg 2's last frame. Actions: pianist plays, notation paints itself.*
*(This is your reference image, brought to life.)*

> **The camera continues the same slow, steady forward glide** between the parting
> mural panels into a composer's studio stage-box. ⟨PREAMBLE⟩ A faceted paper-sculpture
> composer sits at a folded-paper grand piano at center stage, bonsai pines at the
> wings, four paper lanterns glowing amber, ink-wash mountains rising across the back
> mural. As the composer's folded hands move over the keys, sepia notation paints
> itself across a long manuscript scroll unfurling from the piano's music stand. A
> slow half-orbit around the piano, keeping the composer centered, then continuing
> past. **In the final second the camera settles back into a slow, steady forward
> glide following the unfurling manuscript scroll toward the room's glowing side
> arch.**

## LEG 4 — The Approval (golden hour begins)
*Start image: Leg 3's last frame. Actions: figures review, seal presses.*

> **The camera continues the same slow, steady forward glide** as the manuscript
> scroll leads through the arch into a review room stage-box, its murals now washed
> in early golden light. ⟨PREAMBLE⟩ Two faceted paper figures stand relaxed at a
> cardstock table, a neatly bound presentation of layered pages between them, tiny
> paper cups nearby. One figure presses a round crimson paper seal onto the top
> page — the watercolor ink of the seal blooms softly outward into the paper fibers.
> Relief, not celebration; the lanterns warm slightly. **In the final second the
> camera settles back into a slow, steady forward glide toward the sealed pages as
> their corners begin to lift and fold.**

## LEG 5 — The Delivery (deep golden hour)
*Start image: Leg 4's last frame. Actions: package folds together, camera surveys.*

> **The camera continues the same slow, steady forward glide** as the sealed pages
> fold with crisp origami precision into an elegant delivery chest of layered
> cardstock at center stage. ⟨PREAMBLE⟩ Around it, blank version cards rise in tidy
> ranks from the tabletop, ribbon-tied document bundles slide square, small folders
> stack themselves, and a long paper ribbon embossed with a gentle waveform relief
> threads the arrangement together, lantern light catching the gold flecks. The
> camera glides low across the perfect arrangement — effortless organization.
> **In the final second the camera settles back into a slow, steady forward glide
> rising gently toward the wide moon-gate window glowing gold at the back.**

## LEG 6 — The Hero (dusk, lanterns as stars)
*Start image: Leg 5's last frame. Actions: pull back, world settles.*

> **The camera continues the same slow, steady forward glide, rising and slowly
> pulling back** through the golden moon-gate into a wide reveal: all the stage-box
> rooms — conference room, gallery, studio, review room, delivery table — standing
> together as one connected paper-theater sculpture, lanterns glowing like warm
> stars against dusk-washed ink-wash mountains. ⟨PREAMBLE⟩ At the center of the wide
> shot a tall blank parchment panel stands softly lit, like a cinema screen at dusk.
> The torn-paper foreground layers settle; gold flecks catch the last light. **The
> camera comes to a slow, serene rest facing the blank parchment panel.**

## 0 · THE TRUST LAW (now the top gate, above all aesthetics)

Every scene answers one question: **"Why should an Executive Producer trust us
with a campaign?"**
- Every animation must **reduce uncertainty**.
- Every transition must **communicate progress**.
- Every interaction must **teach the product**.
- **Beauty is the byproduct, not the objective.** A beautiful interaction that
  does not increase trust, understanding, or confidence is removed — no appeal.
The ship-gate checklist gains this as item #0, checked first.

## 1 · THE SIGNATURE, COMPLETED — the explosion now lands in THEIR folder

The stem explosion was missing its ending: it demonstrated craft, then
reassembled into *our* master. It now resolves into *the client's outcome*.

**The full sequence (Beat 3, revised):**

1. **MASTER** — the tape, center, breathing. *"This is a delivery. Touch it."*
2. **STEMS** — burst into five living strips (as scripted).
3. **THE TURN (new):** the stems don't reassemble — they **file themselves.**
   An **agency folder** materializes beneath (plain, familiar — their world,
   not ours: a folder icon with THEIR placeholder name, `VANCE_HOLIDAY_2026/`).
   Then, in tempo, the delivery *builds itself into the folder* as a visible
   tree:
   - `01_MUSIC/` — master + stems + cutdowns glide in as named files
   - `02_RIGHTS/` — the certificate flies in, seal stamping ON the beat
   - `03_CUE_SHEET/` — the PDF folds itself in
   - `04_VERSIONS/` — v1 · v2 · v3 FINAL stack like tape spines
   - `05_DELIVERY_MANIFEST/` — the checklist writes, each line ticking
4. **CAMPAIGN READY** — the folder closes with a soft, physical *thunk*; a
   single stamp lands across it: **CAMPAIGN READY**. Copy beneath:
   *"This lands in your drive. Organized like this. Every time."*

**Why this wins:** the previous ending said *we make beautiful masters*. This
ending says ***your Friday is already organized*** — the differentiation is the
folder, because no music house on earth demos their file hygiene, and file
hygiene is exactly what an EP's trust is made of.

**MOTION.** Filing = match-moves (strip → file row, certificate → folder slot),
1/16 staggers, spring settles; the manifest lines tick on-beat; the thunk is a
2-frame contact hold + 3% frame dip. Reverse scroll re-opens the folder
(explorable, not a movie). Touch: pinned scrub, 2.5 viewports.

## 2 · THE IMPOSSIBLE MOMENT — **"THE LISTENING MACHINE"** (Beat 4 opener)

*Chosen over "proposal writes itself" and pure "waveform→metadata" because it
fuses both and is the truest demo of the product's intelligence.*

**THE SHOT.** A real waveform spans the frame, playing (sound-on) or scrubbed
by scroll (sound-off). As the playhead crosses the wave, **spoken words lift
out of the waveform itself** — rising from the exact peaks that "said" them —
drift upward, and **crystallize into Campaign Intelligence fields** that
assemble live at the top of the frame:

> wave … *"somewhere between eighteen and twenty-four"* → the words rise →
> snap into **`BUDGET · $18–24k`**
> wave … *"it has to be live in November"* → **`DEADLINE · November`**
> wave … *"a sixty, cutdowns, and we'll need stems"* →
> **`DELIVERABLES · :60 + cutdowns + stems`**

Each crystallized field stays **tethered to the wave** by a hairline dropping to
the exact timecode it came from — hover a field and its second of waveform
re-lights (sound-on: replays). The machine's understanding is *traceable to the
breath it heard.*

Copy, after the third field lands: **"Your discovery call becomes the spec.
Automatically. With receipts."**

**Why it's impossible-looking:** type appearing to be *emitted by audio*, in
sync, with provenance threads — developers will ask how. (How: pre-timed
transcript keyed to the waveform, one canvas, letters as particles following a
rise-curve, fields as spring targets. Looks like magic; ships at 60fps.)

**Why it's allowed:** it teaches the realest thing ChordOS does (transcript →
Campaign Intelligence with provenance), reduces the EP's biggest uncertainty
("will I have to re-explain everything?"), and its beauty is entirely byproduct.

## 3 · PER-BEAT CAMERA SPEC SHEETS

*(LENS: parallax/FOV preset · LIGHT: key behavior · FRAME: composition ·
PACE: camera speed · TEMPO: what sits on the grid · GRADE: color ·
DEPTH: plane count & separation)*

**BEAT 1 · FRIDAY (0–3s)**
LENS 85mm, shallow (fore/back blur 6px) · LIGHT key absent — cold spill only,
10% under-exposure · FRAME fragments rule-of-thirds edges, thesis dead center ·
PACE static camera, handheld ±2px · TEMPO fragments OFF-grid (±90ms), thesis ON
beat 5 · GRADE desaturated ink, warmth removed (-15 temp) · DEPTH 2 planes,
minimal — claustrophobia is flat.

**BEAT 2 · SOMEONE GETS IT (3–7s)**
LENS 35mm opening up · LIGHT the key arrives — warm sweep L→R lights wordmark;
record light blooms on · FRAME wordmark lower-third left, copy center-left,
whitespace right (calm = room) · PACE handheld decays to 0 over one bar; push
begins 2%/bar · TEMPO settle ON the bar; nav arpeggio 1/16s · GRADE warmth
returns (+10 temp, ember lift in shadows) · DEPTH 3 planes (stand / type /
air), 1.4×–0.6×.

**BEAT 3 · EVIDENCE — the signature**
LENS 35mm orbit ±10°; 85mm INSERT for the seal stamp · LIGHT single top-left
key; stems self-lit (earned bloom); folder matte until stamped · FRAME master
center on reflection floor; folder builds lower-third; manifest right column ·
PACE orbit pointer-damped (spring 0.8); filing pass one bar per folder row ·
TEMPO burst on beat, files 1/16s, thunk on the downbeat · GRADE full warm
studio; certificate paper +5 exposure · DEPTH 4 planes (air / stems / master /
floor reflection).

**BEAT 4 · THE LISTENING MACHINE + THE SYSTEM**
LENS 35mm locked-off (the one static camera — testimony, not spectacle) ·
LIGHT playhead is the light source; fields glow on crystallize; panels rim-lit ·
FRAME wave lower half, fields assemble top-third, tethers connect · PACE scroll
= playhead scrub 1:1, honest · TEMPO words lift at true speech timing (the ONE
off-grid element — human speech), fields SNAP on-grid (understanding = order) ·
GRADE ink + ember playhead; fields in spec-mono cream · DEPTH 3 planes (fields /
tethers / wave).

**BEAT 5 · YOUR WEEK, HANDLED**
LENS 50mm equivalent (neutral, factual) · LIGHT answers pre-lit, fragments
unlit; rack = light handoff · FRAME problem upper-left small, answer center
large · PACE steady dolly, one station per viewport · TEMPO fragments off-grid,
dissolves ON beat · GRADE fragments keep Beat-1 cold; answers full warm — the
grade itself argues · DEPTH 2 planes, deliberate flatness (clarity).

**BEAT 6 · THE INVITATION**
LENS 35mm, wide, still · LIGHT room at idle: key low, record light breathing
(half-tempo), CTA bloom on approach only · FRAME symmetric, generous margins ·
PACE camera dead still — the only fully static scene end-to-end · TEMPO breath
= whole notes · GRADE warmest frame of the film (+12 temp) · DEPTH 3 planes at
rest, parallax ≤1% (presence without motion).

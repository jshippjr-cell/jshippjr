# Product Spec: Clearance-Certified Campaign Music

*One-page spec — Chordential beachhead product (Roadmap A)*
*Status: draft · Owner: Chordential · Date: 2026-06-16*

## The one-liner

**Fully-owned, guaranteed-clearable custom music for brand campaigns —
delivered fast, with cutdowns and stems, and a certificate your legal team can
defend.** The thing AI-generated music legally cannot sell.

## Problem

As AI floods the market with cheap music (~44% of daily uploads are now
AI-generated), buyers face the opposite of scarcity for *commodity* tracks — and
acute scarcity of music they can actually **license, monetize, insure, and
defend**. Two specific pains:

1. **Clearance uncertainty.** AI-only output is not copyrightable (US Copyright
   Office, Jan 2025), so it cannot be cleanly licensed or indemnified. Buyers
   cannot tell what is safe.
2. **The cross-channel license trap.** Platform in-app libraries only cover
   native posts; the moment a brand reposts cross-channel or puts paid spend
   behind a video, the blanket license dies and Content ID claims divert revenue.

## Target customer

- **Primary:** ad agencies and production companies (reseller channel — they buy
  repeatedly and bring their own clients).
- **Secondary:** direct brand marketing/creative teams running social-first
  campaigns.

## The product: three packages

| Package | Scope | Turnaround | Price (anchor) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Social Sprint** | 1 original track + 3 cutdowns (:06/:15/:30) + stems | 72 hrs | ~$2,500 |
| **Campaign** | Hero track + up to 5 cutdowns + stems + alt mixes | 5–7 days | ~$6,000 |
| **Brand Anthem** | Signature theme + sonic logo + cutdown kit + stems | 2–3 weeks | ~$15,000 |

Pricing sits **above royalty-free subscriptions, below major-label sync** — the
$2k–25k regional/digital band is the natural ceiling for agency custom packages.

## The differentiator: the Clearance Certificate

Every delivery ships with a one-page certificate guaranteeing:

- **Full ownership / clean chain of title** (human-authored, work-for-hire or
  assigned, no uncleared samples or AI-only material).
- **Cross-channel + paid-spend license** — the license follows the asset across
  every platform and behind paid media (closes the in-app-library trap).
- **Content-ID safelist registration** — Chordential's owned music is whitelisted
  so it never generates claims against the buyer's channels.
- **Indemnification** — Chordential stands behind the clearance.

This is the moat: trust, speed, and a defensible contract — not catalog scale.

## What's explicitly out of scope (v1)

- No stock/library catalog (no catalog-scale play; this is bespoke).
- No third-party/major-label sync brokering.
- No self-serve platform yet — sales are relationship-led.

## Operating model (how it stays fast and profitable)

- **Standardized workflow:** intake brief → scope lock → single revision round
  (capped) → certified delivery. Revision caps kill scope creep.
- **Deliver longest version first**, then cut down — the efficient way to produce
  multi-version assets.
- **Pricing structure:** start project-based, convert repeat agencies to
  **monthly retainers** for predictable MRR.

## Go-to-market

1. Pitch 10–15 target agencies/production companies; land 3–5 pilots.
2. Lead with the AI-anxiety hook: *"music your legal team can defend."*
3. Publish clearance / Content-ID educational content as inbound.
4. Convert pilots to retainers; add a partner portal and one composer-contractor
   to scale delivery.

## Success metrics

| Gate | Metric |
|---|---|
| MVP complete | 3 packages + Clearance Certificate template live |
| Launch | 3 paying pilot agencies |
| Growth | 5 agencies / ~$15k MRR; >50% revenue from retainers |

## Risks & mitigations

- **AI gets "licensed" and clearable (2026 label deals).** → Compete on speed,
  bespoke fit, and human authorship; certificate still covers chain of title.
- **Project lumpiness.** → Push retainers; fund the recurring-revenue subscription
  product (Roadmap C) from this cash flow.
- **Clearance liability.** → Strict work-for-hire paperwork, no uncleared samples,
  documented provenance for every asset.
